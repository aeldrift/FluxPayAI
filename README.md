# FluxPayAI
An Intelligent AI-powered financial platform delivering smart payment routing, spending insights, fraud detection, and predictive financial analytics.

# Key Features

## 1. Multi-Level Budget & Spending Targets
- Set daily, weekly, and monthly spending targets.
- Create category-wise budgets for food, shopping, travel, entertainment, subscriptions, and other expenses.
- Track spending through a wallet-style dashboard with remaining balances, budget utilisation, and overspending alerts.
- Customise spending categories and limits according to personal financial goals.

## 2. Payment-Linked Budget Tracker
- Integrate with supported payment providers to receive transaction confirmations.
- Automatically record eligible transactions and update spending totals.
- Deduct confirmed expenses from relevant category budgets.
- Display remaining budgets and transaction histories in near real time.
- Handle pending, failed, refunded, and reversed transactions appropriately.
- Prevent duplicate transactions from being counted more than once.
- Support PayPal Sandbox transactions for demonstration and testing.

## 3. AI Financial Copilot
- Analyse spending habits, budgets, recurring expenses, and financial goals.
- Answer natural-language questions about expenses and available budgets.
- Identify overspending trends and categories where users could save money.
- Generate daily, weekly, and monthly financial summaries.
- Provide personalised recommendations to improve spending and saving habits.

## 4. AI Roast Engine
- Generate witty, personalised roasts based on spending habits and purchase patterns.
- Deliver humorous feedback when users overspend or make frequent impulse purchases.
- Adapt the tone and intensity of roasts to user preferences.
- Combine humour with useful financial insights and suggestions.
- Encourage mindful spending without shaming users or restricting their financial choices.

## 5. Impulse-Purchase Intervention
- Provide optional interventions before users complete a purchase.
- Offer customisable cooling-off periods, such as waiting 10 minutes or 24 hours.
- Show how a potential purchase affects daily, weekly, and monthly budgets.
- Suggest cheaper alternatives where relevant data is available.
- Provide personalised pre-purchase nudges based on spending habits and savings goals.
- Allow users to proceed with their purchases whenever they choose.

## 6. MoneyMirror — Financial Future Simulator
- Simulate possible financial outcomes using income, spending patterns, recurring commitments, and savings goals.
- Let users explore hypothetical financial decisions through natural-language questions.
- Estimate how purchases or spending reductions could affect future savings.
- Evaluate affordability for planned purchases based on projected budgets.
- Compare financial scenarios and display their potential outcomes.
- Forecast potential savings over three, six, or twelve months.

**Example queries:**
- "What if I spend ₹5,000 on shopping this month?"
- "What if I cancel two subscriptions?"
- "Can I afford a ₹20,000 purchase next month?"
- "How much could I save in six months if I reduce food delivery?"

## 7. AutoPay Intelligence
- Remind users before scheduled recurring payments and subscription renewals when reliable billing-date information is available.
- Display upcoming automatic payments in a unified dashboard.
- Calculate projected monthly and annual subscription costs.
- Flag unusual changes in recurring charges and potential overlapping subscriptions.
- Forecast how upcoming payments will affect available budgets.
- Highlight subscriptions users may want to review or cancel.
- Provide cancellation guidance or supported management options, subject to available permissions and provider integrations.

## 8. Anonymous-User Fraud Detection
- Analyse suspicious transaction patterns involving anonymous or unidentified users.
- Detect unusual payment frequency, repeated failed attempts, abnormal transaction amounts, and potentially suspicious behaviour.
- Generate risk scores and alerts using available transaction signals.
- Flag high-risk transactions for additional verification or review.
- Explain the risk indicators behind each alert.
- Protect user privacy by using only authorised data and applying appropriate safeguards.

## 9. Financial Survival Game
- Turn responsible spending into an interactive financial challenge.
- Offer missions such as staying within a budget, reducing unnecessary purchases, and meeting savings targets.
- Track progress through streaks, points, levels, and achievement badges.
- Unlock rewards after completing challenges, such as digital scratch cards, brand coupons, and promotional offers.
- Partner with brands to sponsor rewards and generate revenue through clearly disclosed advertising and sponsored campaigns.
- Personalise challenges according to users' goals and progress.
- Reward positive financial habits rather than encouraging unnecessary purchases.
