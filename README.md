# FluxPayAI
An Intelligent AI-powered financial platform delivering smart payment routing, spending insights, fraud detection, and predictive financial analytics.

**Think first. Spend smarter.**

SpendRoast is a personal finance companion that watches your spending, tells you the truth about it, and does so with a sense of humour. It tracks your budget in real time, nudges you before you make an impulsive purchase, and roasts you (gently, and only as much as you ask for) when you slip.

Most budgeting apps are accurate and boring, so people quit using them. SpendRoast is built on a simple idea: if checking your budget is fun, you will actually do it.

---

## Why we built this

We have all done it. A "quick" coffee, a late-night order, a sale you didn't need. The regret shows up an hour later, when it can't change anything.

SpendRoast moves that moment of reflection to *before* you pay. It never locks you out of your own money. It just makes sure you notice what you're about to do.

> "You and Castro can survive without overpriced coffee, but sure, caffeine is a survival necessity, no?"

## What it does

### Budgets and spending targets
Set daily, weekly and monthly limits, and split them into categories like food, shopping, travel, entertainment and subscriptions. A wallet-style dashboard shows what's left, how much of each budget you've used, and warns you when you're overspending. Categories and limits are yours to change.

### Payment-linked tracking
Connect a supported payment provider and your budget updates itself whenever a payment is confirmed. No manual entry needed.

- Confirmed expenses are deducted from the right category.
- Pending, failed, refunded and reversed payments are handled properly.
- Duplicate transactions are never counted twice.
- PayPal Sandbox is supported for demos and testing.

### AI Financial Copilot
Ask questions in plain language, like "How much do I have left for food this week?" The copilot looks at your habits, recurring expenses and goals, points out where you're overspending, and sends daily, weekly and monthly summaries with practical suggestions.

### AI Roast Engine
Witty, personal feedback based on how you actually spend. You choose the tone and intensity, from a light nudge to a proper roasting. Every roast comes with something useful, and the goal is to encourage mindful spending, not to shame you or take away your choices.

### Impulse-Purchase Intervention
Before a purchase goes through, SpendRoast can step in with a few options:

- **Buy it anyway.** It's your money, always.
- **Wait.** Pick a cooling-off period, from 10 minutes to 24 hours.
- **Find a cheaper alternative**, where we have the data to suggest one.

It also shows how the purchase affects your daily, weekly and monthly budgets, so you see the real cost before you decide.

### MoneyMirror: Financial Future Simulator
Ask "what if" and see where it leads. MoneyMirror uses your income, spending, recurring commitments and goals to estimate outcomes over three, six or twelve months.

- "What if I spend ₹5,000 on shopping this month?"
- "What if I cancel two subscriptions?"
- "Can I afford a ₹20,000 purchase next month?"
- "How much could I save in six months if I reduce food delivery?"

You can also compare scenarios side by side.

### AutoPay Intelligence
Subscriptions are easy to forget. This feature collects your upcoming automatic payments in one place, reminds you before renewals (when billing dates are reliably available), and shows what your subscriptions cost per month and per year. It flags unusual price changes and overlapping services, and helps you decide what to review or cancel.

### Anonymous-User Fraud Detection
Looks for suspicious patterns around anonymous or unidentified users: unusual payment frequency, repeated failed attempts, strange amounts. Each transaction gets a risk score, high-risk ones are flagged for extra verification, and every alert explains *why* it was raised. Only authorised data is used, with privacy safeguards in place.

### Financial Survival Game
Saving money, turned into a game. Take on missions like "Survive today on ₹500 instead of ₹1,000," stay within a category budget, or hit a savings target. Earn streaks, points, levels and badges, and unlock rewards such as scratch cards and brand coupons. Challenges adapt to your goals and progress.

Rewards are designed to back good habits, not to push you into buying more. Sponsored rewards and campaigns from brand partners are always clearly labelled as such.

## Principles

- **You're in control.** We nudge, we never block.
- **Funny, not cruel.** Humour is the hook, but the point is always to help.
- **Honest numbers.** Your balances should be right, including when payments fail, reverse or get refunded.
- **Your data stays yours.** We use only what we're authorised to use.
